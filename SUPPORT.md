# Support

perception-is-how-we-experience is maintained by one person. This is what that supports.

- **Questions and bugs:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/zachshallbetter/perception-is-how-we-experience/issues/new/choose).
- **Response time:** best-effort, with no SLA. There is no guaranteed response time.
- **Security reports:** do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Versions:** the repository has a tagged release; fixes land on `main` and are not backported to older tags.
