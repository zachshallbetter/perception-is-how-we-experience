# Smart Home

**Status**
- Label: **Conceptual**
- Evidence status: not yet promoted
- Patent relevance: medium

## Concept

Use room context, audio, occupancy, wearables, contact sensors, and environmental signals as semantically distinct but related streams.

## Why It Matters

This domain is a good live deployment path for:
- ambient sensing
- asynchronous data arrival
- partial modality failure
- bounded adaptation
