# Early Fusion vs Towers

**Status**
- Label: **Conceptual**
- Relation to current repo: partially supported by the completed synthetic canonical-vs-merged routing ladder
- Evidence status: synthetic perception-side evidence is favorable to typed routing overall
- Patent relevance: low to medium

## Concept

Early fusion collapses heterogeneous modalities before they are encoded in their own semantic spaces. Tower-first designs preserve local modality structure first and fuse later in a shared core.

## Why It Matters

Tower-first designs are expected to be better for:

- graceful degradation
- modality extension
- continual learning replay
- interpretability
- heterogeneous sensor scaling

## Relation to Current Repo

Evidenced today:
- the synthetic `sensor_streaming` ladder compares typed canonical routing against a merged stream and favors typed routing overall

Not evidenced today:
- broad real-domain superiority across many deployment domains
