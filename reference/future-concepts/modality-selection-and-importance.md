# Modality Selection and Importance

**Status**
- Label: **Conceptual**

- Evidence status: not yet promoted

## Concept

Choose modalities by failure mode coverage, complementarity, cost, and measured contribution rather than by raw count.

## Why It Matters

More modalities are not automatically better. The right set is the one that closes real failure modes with measurable value.
